# Null Pointer — Production Art Asset Manifest

## Contract

This Phase 5A manifest is the handoff contract for external generation, human-finished raster art, reusable source textures, and Unity-authored procedural visuals. It contains **94 production asset entries**. Nothing in this document is approved final art; every `FINAL target` entry still requires provenance recording, human art direction, pixel cleanup, import verification, and in-engine visual QA.

Each row explicitly supplies: asset ID; purpose and scene/system; priority; final/temporary status; pixel dimensions and aspect; alpha; visual description and composition; camera/view; lighting and palette; variants/frames and consistency reference; output filename and Unity destination; texture type; PPU; pivot; sorting layer/order; independent replacement; dependencies; and AI-generation notes.

Global rules unless a row is stricter:

- PNG, sRGB, straight alpha, no premultiplied fringe. World sprites are 16 PPU; UI/icons are 100 PPU.
- World pixel art uses Point filter, no mipmaps, uncompressed import, no NPOT scaling. UI follows the same pixel discipline unless a reviewed high-resolution text/vector exception is documented.
- `Reference` items are not shipped and go under `Assets/Art/Reference/`; production sprites go only to the listed final paths.
- AI notes `REF-EREN`, `REF-EREN-APT`, `REF-MERT-APT`, `REF-UI`, and `REF-MEM` mean use the approved master sheet/palette from that family and do not re-invent proportions, perspective, or light direction.
- Order ranges follow `ART_BIBLE.md`. `Independent Y` means the asset may be replaced without requiring simultaneous code/gameplay changes; companion lighting or animation frames may still be listed as dependencies.
- Animated sheets always state **per-frame size, frame count, columns, rows, total sheet dimensions, playback rate/duration, and Unity horizontal-flip policy**. Atlases that contain non-playing variants state cell size/count/grid and explicitly say `no playback`.

### Asset-type registry

Every manifest ID has exactly one production type:

- **A — FINAL RASTER ART:** a unique, final illustrated image or sprite delivered by the external/human art pipeline. Every manifest ID not explicitly listed as B or C below is Type A.
- **B — REUSABLE TEXTURE / MASK / ATLAS:** `NP-PROP-EREN-CABLES-001`, `NP-PROP-EREN-CLUTTER-001`, `NP-PROP-EREN-LAMP-001`, `NP-PROP-MERT-NOTES-001`, `NP-PROP-MERT-CABLES-001`, `NP-PROP-MERT-CLUTTER-001`, `NP-MENU-FX-FOG-001`, `NP-MENU-FX-NOISE-001`, every `NP-UI-*` atlas/parts/focus/selection/separator/tabs/card/terminal/warning/scrollbar/slider/icons/glyph entry, every `NP-FX-MEM-*` entry, `NP-FX-RAIN-STREAK-001`, `NP-FX-RAIN-DROP-001`, `NP-FX-PARTICLE-001`, and `NP-TRANSITION-MASK-001`. These are small reusable inputs; Unity controls motion/state.
- **C — UNITY-PROCEDURAL VISUAL:** `NP-MENU-FX-RAIN-001`, `NP-FX-EREN-SCREEN-001`, `NP-FX-EREN-REFLECT-001`, `NP-FX-EREN-RAIN-001`, and `NP-CHAPTER-END-001`. These IDs have **no external raster deliverable**. They are assembled in Unity from referenced Type B inputs, layout masks, materials, particles, UV/RectTransform movement, and controller timing.

### First production batches and approval gates

1. Generate and approve `NP-CHR-EREN-REF-001` first. It is the locked identity source; no gameplay Eren raster may enter generation before this approval.
2. Generate and approve `NP-CHR-EREN-SIDE-001` against the master. Only then generate `NP-CHR-EREN-IDLE-001` and `NP-CHR-EREN-WALK-001` as one consistency batch. Idle/walk must share the same 32×64 body box, baseline, pivot, palette, handedness, and silhouette.
3. The first environment batch is `NP-ENV-EREN-CITY-001`, `NP-ENV-EREN-WINDOW-001`, `NP-ENV-EREN-ARCH-001`, and `NP-ENV-EREN-ROOM-001`. Approve the 480×270 room composition/camera and walk-plane guide before generation; city establishes exterior depth, architecture establishes room geometry, window must match the city opening, and room/floor must match architecture plus the collision guide.
4. These gates lock consistency, not a new direction. The cyber-noir palette, pixel discipline, side-view camera, motivated lighting, and restrained cyan/amber/violet/red grammar in `ART_BIBLE.md` remain authoritative.

## Delivery Acceptance

An asset is not promoted from staging until:

1. Its ID, filename, dimensions, alpha, view, light direction, PPU, pivot, slicing, layer, and order match the row.
2. The AI-generation record includes tool/model/version, date, prompt, all input/reference ownership, license terms, and human edits.
3. A human approves anatomy, perspective, material logic, narrative spoilers, silhouette, palette, pixel cleanup, tiling/seams, and transparency.
4. Unity import inspection and `QA_VISUAL.md` pass at 1920×1080, 2560×1440, and representative 16:10.
5. Assignment occurs through a matching `FinalArtSlot` when the object is builder-owned; manual references are never placed directly on disposable generated objects.
